# Eight field skills: source intake and offline publishing

## Purpose
Maintain eight practical, freely accessible skills: (1) hunt, fish, and harvest; (2) purify water; (3) natural medicine literacy and first aid; (4) shelter; (5) gardening; (6) small-scale power; (7) nonviolent self-defense; (8) navigation.

These eight cards live in `data/guides.js`, so the CodeBound web search, printable standalone HTML, plain-text fieldbook, machine-readable JSON, and portable offline ZIP all derive from the **same editable source**. The field cards are introductory learning checklists, **not certifications** or permission to undertake hazardous procedures. Existing water, garden and shelter system cards remain the deeper dependency plans.

## Responsibilities across the ecosystem
- **CodeBound:** original, source-attributed educational field cards, printable lessons and measurable practice drills.
- **PhiARK:** optional operator-selected original reference files, provenance, hashes and licensing record. Its catalog is not evidence that a file has been downloaded.
- **PhiSafe:** private family/pet inventory and evacuation/readiness exercises. Do not push its private data into public repositories.

Status as of 2026-11-13: **discovery candidate only**. The search results require JavaScript in the current inspection environment; no individual title, author, rights statement or file bytes were independently confirmed here. Do not claim that this collection is vetted, public-domain, medically safe, available to download, or authorized for wholesale mirroring.

Before accepting *any* item:
1. Record canonical Internet Archive item ID, direct item URL, exact title, creator, publication/edition date, actual file format and page count.
2. Document copyright/license/permission separately from mere availability. A readable PDF is not automatically free to redistribute.
3. Identify the specific useful teaching claim. For high-consequence topics, compare against contemporary primary health, land-management, engineering or professional standards.
4. Log hazards, contradictions, local-law requirements, audience and the needed subject-matter reviewer. Label unreviewed entries **community-draft / unverified source**.
5. If an operator legitimately obtains a file, import it explicitly into PhiARK with the exact URL, provenance and license status; record its hash. **Never silently download or mirror an entire collection.**
6. Test standalone readability, offline search/print, source provenance, and an actual low-risk field exercise. A checksum proves only byte integrity, not factual correctness or rights.
7. Re-review as regulations, medical guidance, hazards and equipment specifications change; retain previous editions for traceability.

Suggested item record fields: `pillar`, `archive_identifier`, `canonical_url`, `title`, `creator`, `edition_date`, `media_type`, `rights_basis`, `sha256_if_local`, `claims_checked`, `primary_sources`, `safety_review`, `reviewer`, `reviewed_at`, `offline_pack_id`.

## First low-risk practice sessions
| Pillar | Practice | Pass/fail evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Hunt/fish/harvest | Look up current local regulations and fish advisories, identify five common local species from trusted field guides **without harvesting** | Species, location-specific rules and advisories recorded |
| Water | Label safe/untreated water containers and rehearse CDC decision flow with clean tap water | Correct biological vs chemical hazard distinction |
| Natural medicine | Compare three plant claims with NIH NCCIH safety summaries **without ingestion** | Interactions and uncertain evidence flagged |
| Shelter | Pitch a commercial tarp/tent at a safe permitted practice site | Weather protection, exits and CO hazards checked |
| Garden | Start a labeled test tray with edible vegetable seed | Germination dates, soil/water and observations recorded |
| Power | Measure a phone/light/radio power budget, test approved portable charger | Watts × hours and measured runtime recorded |
| Self-defense | Walk through retreat, boundary setting and contact plans | No confrontation or traps used |
| Navigation | Use a paper topo map and compass on a short known safe route | Bearings and return checkpoints correctly recorded |

## Publication and safety gates
- Preserve CodeBound's policy: no weapon-making, offensive tactics, unqualified electrical construction, unverified medical treatment, or unsupported potability claims.
- High-consequence steps require primary references and eventual independent specialist review before publication as trusted procedures.
- Leave guides accessible with no account, telemetry, subscription or connectivity after deliberate offline export.
- Every training exercise should be safe, repeatable, voluntary and available in printable text.
