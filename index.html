03 / THE WORKBENCH

Tools that work without a cloud.

Simple code, clear inputs, and local data ownership. Keep a paper backup too, because browsers are not magical indestructible vaults.

TOOL_01CALCULATOR

Emergency water baseline

CDC starting guidance: at least one US gallon per person per day for three or more days. This estimates a minimum for people only.

Add water for pets, illness, hot weather, extra hygiene, and local conditions. This tool does not test water quality. CDC source ↗

TOOL_02LOCAL LEDGER

The commons supply ledger

Log shared supplies on this device. Nothing is uploaded. Export JSON regularly and avoid sensitive personal data.
